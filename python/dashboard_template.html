The question and the boundary

Which international food benchmarks show price pressure, how predictable are their next-month prices, and how would assumed price and FX shocks affect a buyer's budget?

Benchmark prices are nominal USD, standardized to USD per metric ton. They are not local retail prices, local inflation, country-level hunger measures or company purchase costs.

Methods you can explain in an interview

1. Clean, normalize and reconcile

Read Monthly Prices explicitly, trim headers, parse calendar months, select eight series from January 2000. Reject duplicate keys, missing months, nonpositive prices and missing selected prices. Sugar and cocoa convert from USD/kg to USD/metric ton by multiplying by 1,000. SQL lag results are checked against Python.

2. Price changes and shock detection

MoM = price / price one month ago − 1; YoY = price / price 12 months ago − 1. Shock z-score compares this month's log return to the mean and standard deviation of the preceding 36 returns. Upward shock ≥ 2; downward shock ≤ −2. Thresholds are exploratory rules, not validated crisis probabilities. Twelve-month volatility = sample standard deviation of monthly log returns × √12.

3. Forecasts, validation and uncertainty

Compare last observed price, the same month last year, and ridge regression on log-price lags 1, 2, 3, 12 plus trailing 3- and 12-month means. Ridge uses up to 120 prior target months, training-only scaling and fixed alpha 10. Validate on 24 months immediately before the last 24 months. Lock the lowest validation MAE model, then evaluate on the final 24. Refit to forecast the next month. The range uses the 80th percentile of absolute validation log residuals. This is empirical calibration, not guaranteed future coverage.

4. Evidence, interpretation and limitations

Price patterns establish association and timing, not causes. A high price level can coexist with a negative YoY change. Local currencies, subsidies, freight and contracts affect local transmission. The included source workbook has an ancillary Mismatch Details sheet; the original bytes are preserved, analysis uses Monthly Prices, and independent verification of each benchmark has not been performed.
